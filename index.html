Free ways to use WhatsApp

Most efficient: one WhatsApp group per ministry or event. Tap Share to a WhatsApp group to post the programme, add a WhatsApp poll for Yes or No, then use the Ask list only for people who have not replied. All of this is free.

Also free: the one-to-one WhatsApp buttons (you press Send), WhatsApp Communities for organising several groups, and Channels for announcements with no replies.

Not recommended: Meta's automatic business service. It charges per message, and from 1 October 2026 it started charging for replies that used to be free in the 24-hour window. Reports differ on whether a small monthly allowance remains, so it can't be relied on as free.

Add people to groups only with their agreement. WhatsApp's rules and prices can change, so check the app for current options.

Your data and privacy
